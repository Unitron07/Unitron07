# Unitron07

I build Windows game-streaming tools and Android apps, and explore phone-based tracking and VR hardware.

## Apps with public downloads

### [Asteria — Windows game streaming](https://github.com/Unitron07/Asteria-Windows)

A Moonlight-derived game-streaming client for Windows x64 and native ARM64, with experimental PyroWave decoding and native Vulkan presentation. Isolated remote sessions and PCVR are planned features.

[Downloads](https://github.com/Unitron07/Asteria-Windows/releases/latest) · [Documentation](https://github.com/Unitron07/Asteria-Windows#readme)

### [Campus Day — offline Android timetable](https://github.com/Unitron07/UofC-Schedule-App)

Import an ICS calendar, see your next class, and keep course details and rooms handy. Includes University of Calgary schedule support; no university login or timetable upload is required in the app. An independent project, unaffiliated with the University of Calgary.

[Android APK](https://github.com/Unitron07/UofC-Schedule-App/releases/latest) · [Features and setup](https://github.com/Unitron07/UofC-Schedule-App#readme) · [Privacy](https://github.com/Unitron07/UofC-Schedule-App/blob/main/PRIVACY.md)

## Experimental projects

### [Astraeus VR — Android ARCore tracking](https://github.com/Unitron07/Astraeus-VR)

Experimental Galaxy S24 6DoF tracking and sensor fusion, sent over UDP to a Windows pose viewer. This is tracking research; a complete VR streaming system and runtime driver are not implemented.

[Build and try it](https://github.com/Unitron07/Astraeus-VR#readme) · [Validation and limitations](https://github.com/Unitron07/Astraeus-VR/blob/main/docs/validation.md)

### [PSVR2-Wireless — feasibility research](https://github.com/Unitron07/PSVR2-Wireless)

Investigating wireless PlayStation VR2 PC use through an Android phone and wearable bridge. Early research and documentation only; no working wireless VR prototype has been demonstrated by this project.

[Research and architecture](https://github.com/Unitron07/PSVR2-Wireless#readme) · [Roadmap](https://github.com/Unitron07/PSVR2-Wireless/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)

Bug reports, reproducible test results and contributions are welcome in the relevant repository.
